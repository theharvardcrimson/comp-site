Are you witty? Funny (be honest now….)? A serial Sidechat user? Want a great
community to laugh and eat Trader Joe's snacks with? Blog is the place (board?)
to be.

The Crimson's flagship blog, Flyby covers all things student life, from
Housing Day to Visitas. We encourage you to write about whatever interests you
most, whether it be HUDS, your Blank Street addiction, or bush jumping — no
topic is too niche or unserious!
