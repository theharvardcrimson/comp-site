The Crimson is financially independent from Harvard — which means the lights
stay on because of the work we do. Business Board members sign five-figure
contracts, produce award-winning sponsored content, and plan international
conferences, all before graduating.

Unlike most Crimson boards, Business comp is competitive. Over the course of
several weeks, compers will participate in two written application rounds,
workshops, coffee chats, and interviews with our executive team. No previous
experience is required — everything you need for the comp, you'll learn
during the comp.
