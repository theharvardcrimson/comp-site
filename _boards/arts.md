Do you care deeply about what we see, watch, and hear? Interested in using art
to reflect on our world and shape vital civic conversations? Arts is for you.

We cover pop culture icons, local concerts, new releases, and the arts scene at
Harvard and across Boston. Our writers have reported from Cannes, interviewed
Will Ferrell and Celeste Ng, and regularly get free tickets to advance movie
screenings, music performances, and speaker events.
