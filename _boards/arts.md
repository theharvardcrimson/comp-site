Seething about the anticlimactic ending of a masterful show? Eager to break down
an incendiary pop culture moment? Excited about an upcoming concert with your
favorite musician? Curious about the team behind Harvard's biggest musical of the
season? Arts is the board for you.

The Arts Board of The Crimson teems with cinephiles and book lovers, TV
aficionados, and theater enthusiasts. Broadly speaking, Arts is a board for
anyone who cares deeply about what we see, watch, and hear, and anyone who wants
to use art as a way of reflecting on our world and shaping vital civic
conversations — and is interested in becoming a community arts reporter, cover
art on campus, in the metro area, and beyond.

Our coverage encompasses topics including [pop culture icons](https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2024/4/9/kwesi-james-try-everything-internet-personality-hollywood-profile/),
[local concerts](https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2024/4/30/the-sinclair-strumbellas-concert-review/),
[newly released art](https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2024/11/19/bleachers-merry-christmas-please-dont-call-single-review/),
and the [important work of local arts leaders](https://www.thecrimson.com/column/underground-overlooked-art-of-the-red-line/article/2023/12/12/underground-overlooked-new-england-decorative-arts/).
We also cover Harvard's arts scene: from [Barry Keoghan's "Man of the Year" celebration](https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2024/2/22/barry-keoghan-man-of-year-hasty-pudding-theatricals-harvard-2024/)
to the [a capella shows](https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2023/11/14/harvardkey-a-veri-secure-jam-veritones-lowkeys-feature/)
to the [the work of student artists](https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2024/12/24/max-allison-25-harvard-art-tech-brain-waves-synthesizer-actor-conflux/).
We have long traditions of covering Boston's biggest cultural institutions: from
the [Boston Symphony Orchestra](https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2024/10/1/Boston-symphony-orchestra-concert-for-the-city-mayor-wu/)
to the [Museum of Fine Arts](https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2024/10/29/georgia-okeeffe-henry-moore-mfa-boston-exhibition/)
to the [Boston Ballet](https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2022/11/29/boston-ballet-2022-as-anticipated-review-article/).

Aside from being a part of the wonderful, idiosyncratic, and outrageously witty
community that is the Arts Board, there are plenty of other perks to comping as
well! Our writers have attended the [Cannes Film Festival](https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2024/9/10/anora-cannes-sean-baker/),
sat down to interview people like [Will Ferrell](https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2012/3/20/casa-de-mi-padre-ferrell/)
and [Celeste Ng](https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2024/3/19/celeste-ng-profile-truth-crowbar-subjective-little-fires-everywhere/),
and regularly get tickets to advance movie screenings, music performances, and
guest speaker events — all free of charge.

We, like all Crimson boards, welcome everyone regardless of prior journalism
experience. Our comp is designed to teach you everything you will need to know as
a writer, from interviewing techniques to crafting the perfect lede. All you need
to do is show up—and write!

One of the coolest parts about the Crimson Arts comp is that when you finish, you
will have five published pieces on the website (and potentially a couple in
print).

Articles are pitched out by editors at our weekly Pitch Meetings, which take
place on Mondays at 7 p.m.

In addition to writing articles and attending Pitch Meetings, compers must attend
our weekly comp seminars at 6 p.m. on Mondays (i.e., directly before Pitch
Meetings). At these seminars we'll teach you everything you need to know about
arts writing, even if you have never written a complete sentence.

Comping another board as well? Talk to your comp directors to learn more about our
policies that may lessen your Arts comp requirements.

Did you start comping Arts but got distracted? Don't sweat it. We've kept track of
everything you've already written and the articles still count towards your comp.

For those of you returning from a prior semester to complete your comp: Come back
soon. We miss you.
